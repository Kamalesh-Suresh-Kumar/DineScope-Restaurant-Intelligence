05 / RESPONSIBLE INTERPRETATION

Good analysis
knows its limits.

Results should be read alongside data quality, modelling assumptions and the scope of the evaluation.

01Dataset-dependent

Predictions and classifications are constrained by the supplied data, feature quality and label distribution.

02Not causal evidence

Statistical tests may identify associations or differences; they do not establish causation.

03Spatial caveats

Restaurant counts are not area-normalised. Mean coordinates are approximate, and DBSCAN clusters are density groupings.

04Rankings have scope

City and locality recommendations depend on project-specific eligibility and scoring criteria.
