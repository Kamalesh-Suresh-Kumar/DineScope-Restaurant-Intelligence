METHODOLOGY AND LIMITATIONS
======================================================================

1. Recommendation scores are composite ranking scores, not predicted customer satisfaction.
2. Component scores use percentile ranking among eligible locations.
3. Restaurant availability uses log-transformed restaurant counts.
4. Cuisine diversity uses the number of distinct cuisine labels where available.
5. Rating coverage measures the proportion of restaurants with available ratings.
6. Currency-specific costs are not compared directly because the dataset contains multiple currencies.
7. A higher recommendation score indicates a stronger match to the configured analytical criteria.
8. Restaurant shortlists are ranked by aggregate rating within the highest-ranked city.
9. Adjusted ratings use Bayesian-style shrinkage toward the global average rating to reduce small-sample bias.
10. Rating prior strength is configurable and currently set to 30.
11. Original average ratings are preserved separately from adjusted average ratings.
12. Rating shrinkage is a ranking adjustment and is not a fully fitted Bayesian hierarchical model.
